## Entry points and approval

`node acceptance/environment/sanity.mjs member|fresh_2fa|fresh_install|shelly` runs one explicitly unscored preparation check. After source is committed cleanly and preparation is complete, `node acceptance/environment/binding.mjs <final-validation-receipt.json>` creates a new read-only `environment-admission.json` plus a separate byte-hash receipt under an owned proposal directory. It binds the evaluator commit and complete files, installed production source/build/package identity, runtime and driver inventories, exact target inventory, protected contract, images/recipes, toolchain, Chromium executable, frozen Shelly archive and members, command/check plan, selectors, limits and retained preparation ledger. Neither the proposal builder nor the preparation helper can approve it.

Only after that review, `node acceptance/environment/qualify.mjs <admission-path> <approval-path>` revalidates the installed adapter's admission guard plus the evaluator commit, clean checkout, all bound files, private helpers and retained preparation inputs. A bounded private fsynced append-only admission ledger is created before runtime loading, approval reads, binding validation and lock acquisition, and before each cycle/fault revalidation. Rejections retain readable supplied hashes, sanitized stage/reason/code and message digest; failure to persist is explicit and blocks dispatch. Wrong root/branch stops before writing. Ledger snapshots include admitted/rejected/incomplete entries separately from scored results. A filesystem lock enforces a single series. A stale lock is retained for explicit ownership reconciliation, never silently removed. Every attempt is durably appended before and after execution; the fixed ten cycle IDs remain the denominator. At most one retry follows a setup/environment failure. Product, fixture, isolation, cancelled, missing evidence and unknown outcomes stop the series. Faults are separate and cannot replace failed cycles. Summaries distinguish complete cycles, first-attempt passes and recovered completions.
